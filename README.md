I'm a **DevOps Engineer** with **6+ years** of hands-on experience designing, implementing, and maintaining cloud-native infrastructure. I specialize in **Azure cloud platforms**, **Kubernetes orchestration**, and **CI/CD automation** to help teams deliver faster, more reliably.
